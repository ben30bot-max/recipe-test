Ham & Cheese Breakfast Quesadilla

Breakfast · Yield: 1 serving

550 calories | 53g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 large tortilla (70g)
  • 1 whole egg (50g)
  • 150g liquid egg whites
  • 85g lean ham, sliced
  • 28g shredded light cheese
  • 30g salsa

Directions

  1. Whisk egg and egg whites; season with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat.
  3. Pour in the eggs and let set for 1–2 minutes.
  4. Add ham and cheese, then fold the tortilla in half.
  5. Cook 2–3 minutes per side until golden and crispy.
