Hot Honey Sriracha Chicken & Veggie Bowl

Dinner · Yield: 1 Serving

410 calories | 32g protein | 16g fat | 34g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 oz. lean ground chicken
  • 1 tablespoon Hot Honey Sriracha sauce
  • 1/2 cup zucchini, sliced thin
  • 1/2 cup bell peppers, diced
  • 1/4 cup shredded carrots
  • 1/4 cup onions, sliced
  • 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 clove garlic, minced

Directions

  1. In a skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat.
  2. Add ground chicken and cook, breaking it up for 6 minutes until fully browned.
  3. Drain excess fat if necessary.
  4. Stir in zucchini, bell peppers, carrots, and onions.
  5. Sauté for 4 minutes until the veggies soften slightly.
  6. Stir in Hot Honey Sriracha sauce and cook for 2 more minutes, allowing the flavors to blend.
  7. Spoon the chicken and vegetable mixture over brown rice.
  8. Top with sliced avocado, and garnish with fresh cilantro if desired.
