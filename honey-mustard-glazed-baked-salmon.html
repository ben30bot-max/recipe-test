Honey Mustard Glazed Baked Salmon

Dinner · Yield: 4 servings

350 calories | 32g protein | 22g fat | 6g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 (6-ounce) salmon fillets
  • 1/3 cup Taste Flavor Co.
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon fresh lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/4 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or foil.
  3. In a small bowl, mix Honey Mustard Sauce, olive oil, lemon juice, garlic powder, and smoked paprika.
  4. Pat salmon fillets dry and place them on the prepared baking sheet.
  5. Season with salt and pepper, then brush the honey mustard glaze generously over each fillet.
  6. Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the salmon is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork.
  7. Serve with roasted asparagus and wild rice for a wholesome meal.
