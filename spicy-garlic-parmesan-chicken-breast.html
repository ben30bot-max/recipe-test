Spicy Garlic Parmesan Chicken Breast

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

320 calories | 31.5g protein | 16g fat | 5.6g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 3 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 1/4 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup Spicy Garlic Parm sauce
  • Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish
  • Grated Parmesan cheese for topping

Directions

  1. Drizzle the chicken breasts with fresh lemon juice and season with salt and pepper on both sides.
  2. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium-high heat.
  3. Add minced garlic and sauté until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  4. Add the seasoned chicken breasts to the skillet and cook until golden brown on both sides and cooked through, about 8 minutes per side.
  5. Reduce the heat to medium-low and pour the Spicy Garlic Parm sauce over the chicken breasts.
  6. Simmer for an additional 5 minutes, allowing the sauce to thicken slightly and coat the chicken evenly.
  7. Transfer the chicken breasts to a serving platter.
  8. Garnish with chopped fresh parsley and grated Parmesan cheese before serving.
