Lemon-Sesame Ginger Glazed Salmon & Broccoli

Lunch · Yield: 2 Servings

340 calories | 36g protein | 17g fat | 11g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 (5 oz) salmon fillets
  • 2 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 tablespoons Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk together Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce, fresh lemon juice, olive oil, sea salt and pepper Toss broccoli with the lemon-sesame ginger sauce mix and spread on a baking sheet.
  3. Place salmon fillets alongside the broccoli.
  4. Brush salmon and broccoli generously with the lemon-sesame ginger sauce mix.
  5. Bake for 15 minutes, or until salmon is cooked through and flakes easily.
