MACRO-FRIENDLY APPETIZERS Avocado Cucumber Rolls with Honey Sriracha & Lime Tahini Sauce

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

368 calories | 5.9g protein | 30g fat | 13g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe avocados
  • 1 large cucumber
  • 1/4 cup shredded carrots
  • 1/4 cup alfalfa sprouts
  • 2 tablespoons toasted sesame seeds
  • 1/2 cup tahini
  • 3 tablespoons fresh lime juice
  • 2 tablespoons Hot Honey Sriracha sauce

Directions

  1. Slice the avocado and cucumber into thin strips.
  2. Lay out a cucumber strip and place an avocado slice on top.
  3. Add a pinch of shredded carrots and alfalfa sprouts on top of the avocado.
  4. Roll up the cucumber strip tightly.
  5. Repeat with the remaining ingredients.
  6. In a small bowl, whisk together tahini, fresh lime juice, Hot Honey Sriracha sauce, ginger, and garlic to make the dipping sauce.
  7. Sprinkle sesame seeds over the avocado cucumber rolls and serve with the dipping sauce.
