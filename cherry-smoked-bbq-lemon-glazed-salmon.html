Cherry Smoked BBQ- Lemon Glazed Salmon

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

293 calories | 34.8g protein | 14.6g fat | 4.7g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 1/4 cup Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce
  • 4 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/4 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • Fresh lemon wedges for serving
  • Chopped fresh parsley for garnish

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  3. Place the salmon fillets on the prepared baking sheet.
  4. In a small bowl, whisk together Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce, fresh lemon juice, sea salt, and pepper until well combined; spread evenly over each salmon fillet.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven for 15 minutes, or until the salmon is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork.
  6. Remove from the oven and transfer the salmon to serving plates.
  7. Garnish with fresh lemon wedges and chopped parsley before serving.
