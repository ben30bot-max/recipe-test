MACRO-FRIENDLY FRIES Baked Cajun Garlic Parmesan Fries

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

300 calories | 6.5g protein | 3.9g fat | 60.1g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 large russet potatoes, cut into thin fries
  • 1 tablespoon Spicy Garlic Parm sauce
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh green onions

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. In a bowl, toss the potato fries with Spicy Garlic Parm sauce, paprika, cayenne pepper, garlic powder, olive oil, and sea salt.
  3. Spread the fries on a baking sheet in a single layer.
  4. Bake for 25 minutes or until golden and crispy.
  5. Garnish with chopped fresh green onions to serve.
