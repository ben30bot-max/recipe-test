Spicy Black Bean & Avocado Flatbread Pizza

Dinner · Yield: 2 Servings

355 calories | 14g protein | 16g fat | 42g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 whole-wheat flatbreads or naan
  • 1/4 cup Southwest Ranch Sauce
  • 1/2 cup black beans, rinsed and mashed slightly
  • 1/4 cup corn kernels
  • 1/4 cup shredded Monterey Jack cheese
  • 1/2 avocado, thinly sliced
  • 1 tablespoon pickled jalapeños
  • 1 tablespoon fresh cilantro, chopped
  • Squeeze of lime juice

Directions

  1. Preheat oven or toaster oven to 400°F.
  2. Place flatbreads on a baking sheet.
  3. Spread a layer of Southwest Ranch Sauce on each.
  4. Top with mashed black beans, corn, and cheese.
  5. Bake for 8-10 minutes until the edges are crispy and cheese is melted.
  6. Remove from oven and immediately top with avocado slices, pickled jalapeños, and cilantro.
  7. Finish with a squeeze of lime juice.
  8. Slice and serve.
