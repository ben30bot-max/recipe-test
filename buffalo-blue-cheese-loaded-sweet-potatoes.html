Buffalo Blue Cheese Loaded Sweet Potatoes

Dinner · Yield: 4 servings

380 calories | 21g protein | 14g fat | 43g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 medium sweet potatoes
  • 1 cup shredded rotisserie chicken
  • 4 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co.
  • 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1/4 cup crumbled blue cheese
  • 2 green onions, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Pierce sweet potatoes with a fork and bake directly on the oven rack for 50 minutes, or until tender.
  3. In a bowl, mix shredded chicken, Buffalo Blue Cheese Sauce, Greek yogurt, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
  4. Once baked, slice each sweet potato open and fluff the insides with a fork.
  5. Spoon the chicken mixture into each sweet potato.
  6. Sprinkle crumbled blue cheese and sliced green onions on top.
  7. Serve hot with a side of fresh greens.
