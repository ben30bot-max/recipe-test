Cherry Smoked BBQ Tilapia Tacos

Dinner · Yield: 1 Serving

330 calories | 23g protein | 10g fat | 35g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 oz. tilapia fillet
  • 2 tablespoons Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce
  • 1 small whole wheat tortilla
  • 1/4 cup shredded cabbage
  • 1 tablespoon Greek yogurt
  • 1 teaspoon fresh lime juice

Directions

  1. Cook tilapia in a skillet over medium heat for 10 minutes until golden brown.
  2. Flake the fish and toss with Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce.
  3. Serve in a tortilla with shredded cabbage, Greek yogurt, and lime juice.
