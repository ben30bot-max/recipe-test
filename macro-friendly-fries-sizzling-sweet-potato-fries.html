MACRO-FRIENDLY FRIES Sizzling Sweet Potato Fries

Dinner · Yield: 2 Servings

200 calories | 0g protein | 7g fat | 38.1g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, cut into fries
  • 2 tablespoons Hot Honey Sriracha sauce
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Toss sweet potato fries with Hot Honey Sriracha sauce and olive oil.
  3. Spread fries on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
  4. Bake for 20-25 minutes or until crispy, turning halfway through.
  5. Sprinkle with sea salt and chopped cilantro before serving.
