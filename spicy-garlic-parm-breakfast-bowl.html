Spicy Garlic Parm Breakfast Bowl

Breakfast · Yield: 1 Serving

320 calories | 9g protein | 14g fat | 40g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup cooked brown rice
  • 1/2 cup roasted sweet potatoes
  • 1/4 cup cooked black beans
  • 1/4 avocado, sliced
  • 1 tablespoon Spicy Garlic Parm sauce
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh cilantro

Directions

  1. In a bowl, layer cooked brown rice, roasted sweet potatoes, and black beans.
  2. Top with avocado slices and drizzle with Spicy Garlic Parm sauce.
  3. Garnish with chopped cilantro.
  4. Mix gently before eating.
  5. Enjoy these flavorful and nutritious breakfasts!
