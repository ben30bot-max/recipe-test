Blueberry Protein French Toast

Breakfast · Yield: 1 serving

510 calories | 59g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 slices (70g) high-protein bread
  • 1 whole egg (50g)
  • 150g liquid egg whites
  • 1 scoop (30g) vanilla protein powder
  • 100g blueberries
  • Cinnamon, optional

Directions

  1. Whisk egg, egg whites, protein powder and cinnamon.
  2. Heat a non-stick pan and lightly spray with cooking spray.
  3. Dip each slice of bread, coating both sides.
  4. Cook 2–3 minutes per side until golden brown.
  5. Top with blueberries and enjoy.
