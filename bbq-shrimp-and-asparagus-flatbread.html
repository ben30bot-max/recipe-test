BBQ Shrimp and Asparagus Flatbread

Dinner · Yield: 2 Servings

365 calories | 35.1g protein | 13g fat | 25.2g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 whole-wheat carb flatbread
  • 1/4 cup Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce
  • 1/2 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1/2 cup asparagus, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup red bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup feta cheese, crumbled

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Spread Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce on the flatbread.
  3. Top with shrimp, asparagus, red bell pepper, and feta cheese.
  4. Bake for 10-12 minutes or until the edges are golden and the cheese is melted.
  5. Slice and serve.
