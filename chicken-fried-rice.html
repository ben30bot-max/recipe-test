10-Minute Chicken Fried Rice

Dinner · Yield: 1 serving

750 calories | 75g protein

Ingredients

  • 7 oz chicken breast
  • 1½ cups cooked jasmine rice
  • 1 whole egg
  • 1 cup frozen peas + carrots
  • Soy sauce
  • Garlic powder

Directions

  1. Cook diced chicken.
  2. Add frozen vegetables + rice.
  3. Crack in the egg.
  4. Add soy sauce.
  5. Stir until hot.
