Zesty Southwest Ranch Black Bean Burgers

Dinner · Yield: 2 servings (1 burger each)

355 calories | 20g protein | 6g fat | 60g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1/4 cup whole-wheat breadcrumbs
  • 1/4 cup finely diced onion
  • 1 large egg white
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 2 tablespoons Southwest Ranch Sauce
  • 2 whole-wheat buns
  • Toppings: lettuce, tomato, onion, avocado

Directions

  1. In a bowl, mash black beans with a fork, leaving some chunks for texture.
  2. Add breadcrumbs, onion, egg white, cumin, and Southwest Ranch Sauce.
  3. Mix until well combined.
  4. Form into two patties.
  5. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat with a light spray of oil.
  6. Cook patties for 5 minutes per side, until firm and browned.
  7. Serve on whole-wheat buns topped with slices of red onion, tomato, lettuce, and avocado.
