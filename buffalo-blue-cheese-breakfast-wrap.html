Buffalo Blue Cheese Breakfast Wrap

Breakfast · Yield: 4 wraps

380 calories | 18g protein | 24g fat | 25g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 large whole-wheat tortillas
  • 4 scrambled eggs
  • 4 bacon slices, cooked and crumbled
  • 4 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co.
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 avocado, sliced

Directions

  1. Lightly warm tortillas in a skillet or microwave.
  2. Spread 2 tablespoons of sauce on each tortilla.
  3. Add scrambled eggs, crumbled turkey bacon, cheddar cheese, and avocado.
  4. Fold in the sides and roll into a wrap.
  5. Serve immediately.
