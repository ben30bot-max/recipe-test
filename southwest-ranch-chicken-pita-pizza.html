Southwest Ranch Chicken Pita Pizza

Lunch · Yield: 1 Servings

340 calories | 32g protein | 4g fat | 30g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 whole wheat pita bread
  • 3 oz cooked chicken breast, shredded
  • 2 tablespoons Southwest Ranch Sauce
  • 2 tablespoons light mozzarella cheese
  • 2 tablespoons diced red onion
  • 2 tablespoons diced tomato
  • 1 tablespoon chopped cilantro

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Spread Southwest Ranch Sauce on pita as the base.
  3. Top with chicken, onion, tomato, and cheese.
  4. Bake for 8–10 minutes until crisp and golden.
  5. Garnish with cilantro before serving.
