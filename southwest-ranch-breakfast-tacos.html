Southwest Ranch Breakfast Tacos

Breakfast · Yield: 2 Servings (2 tacos each)

310 calories | 18g protein | 16g fat | 25g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 small corn tortillas
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1/4 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup diced red bell pepper
  • 2 tablespoons shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 tablespoons Southwest Ranch Sauce
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • Fresh cilantro and lime wedges for garnish

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add bell pepper and tomatoes; sauté for 3 minutes.
  3. Whisk eggs and scramble with black beans until cooked through.
  4. Warm tortillas in a dry pan or microwave.
  5. Fill each tortilla with egg mixture and top with cheese.
  6. Drizzle with Southwest Ranch Sauce, garnish with cilantro, and serve with lime wedges.
