MACRO-FRIENDLY APPETIZERS Cherry Smoked BBQ Veggie Skewers

Dinner · Yield: 1 Serving (4 Skewers)

110 calories | 2g protein | 3g fat | 19g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1 cup bell pepper chunks
  • 1 cup zucchini slices
  • 1/2 cup red onion chunks
  • 2 tablespoons Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar

Directions

  1. Preheat grill or oven broiler.
  2. In a bowl, toss cherry tomatoes, bell pepper, zucchini, and red onion with olive oil, balsamic vinegar, salt, and pepper.
  3. Thread vegetables onto skewers.
  4. Grill or broil for 10 minutes, turning occasionally, until vegetables are tender and slightly charred.
  5. Brush with Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce and serve warm.
