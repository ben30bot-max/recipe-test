Spicy Chicken & Avocado Whole-Wheat Flatbread

Dinner · Yield: 2 Servings

295 calories | 20g protein | 12g fat | 26g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 whole-wheat flatbread
  • 1/2 lb. cooked shredded chicken
  • 1 red onion, finely chopped
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 1/4 cup diced tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons shredded cheese
  • 2 tablespoons Southwest Ranch Sauce
  • Fresh cilantro and jalapeño slices

Directions

  1. Warm the flatbread in a skillet or oven until slightly crisp.
  2. Top with shredded chicken, red onion, avocado, and tomatoes.
  3. Sprinkle cheese and drizzle with Southwest Ranch Sauce.
  4. Add cilantro and jalapeño for garnish and serve hot.
