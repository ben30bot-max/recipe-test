Spicy Honey Sriracha Veggie Omelette

Breakfast · Yield: 1 Serving

210 calories | 20g protein | 10g fat | 10g carbs

Ingredients

  • 3 egg whites
  • 1 whole egg
  • 1 cup spinach, chopped
  • 1/2 bell pepper, diced
  • 1/4 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 tablespoon Hot Honey Sriracha sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1/4 cup shredded low-fat cheese

Directions

  1. Whisk the egg whites and whole egg together in a bowl.
  2. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat.
  4. Add bell peppers and cherry tomatoes, and sauté for 3 minutes.
  5. Add spinach and cook until wilted.
  6. Pour the egg mixture over the vegetables.
  7. Cook until eggs are set, then fold in half.
  8. Drizzle Hot Honey Sriracha sauce over the omelette.
  9. Sprinkle with cheese if desired.
