Cheesy Eggplant Scramble

Breakfast · Yield: 2 Servings

271 calories | 18g protein | 19g fat | 6g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 eggplant, diced
  • 1/4 cup diced red onion
  • 1/4 cup diced green bell pepper
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 tablespoons Southwest Ranch Sauce
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh cilantro

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add onion, eggplant, and bell pepper; cook for 4 minutes.
  3. Pour in beaten eggs and scramble gently.
  4. Stir in cheese and Southwest Ranch Sauce just before eggs finish cooking.
  5. Garnish with cilantro and serve warm.
