Spicy Garlic Parm Zucchini Noodle Shrimp Pasta

Dinner · Yield: 2 Servings

315 calories | 30g protein | 17g fat | 14g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 oz. shrimp (peeled and deveined)
  • 2 cups zucchini noodles
  • 1 tablespoon Spicy Garlic Parm sauce
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon grated parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 cup sautéed spinach

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the shrimp and cook for 3 minutes on each side until they are pink and opaque.
  3. Remove from the pan and set aside.
  4. In the same skillet, sauté the zucchini noodles for 3 minutes until they are just tender but still firm.
  5. Avoid overcooking to prevent sogginess.
  6. Remove and set aside.
  7. In the same skillet, add the spinach and cook for 2 minutes until wilted.
  8. Toss the zucchini noodles with the Spicy Garlic Parm sauce, cooked shrimp, sautéed spinach, and parmesan cheese.
  9. Serve hot, garnished with a sprinkle of extra parmesan if desired.
