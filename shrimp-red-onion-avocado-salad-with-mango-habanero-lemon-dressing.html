Shrimp, Red Onion & Avocado Salad with Mango Habanero-Lemon Dressing

Lunch · Yield: 2 servings

434 calories | 19g protein | 34g fat | 17g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons salted butter, melted
  • 225g raw shrimp, peeled & deveined
  • 1 large avocado, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, minced
  • 1 cup diced cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co. Fiery Mango Habanero Sauce
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Handful chopped cilantro
  • Salt & pepper

Directions

  1. In a small bowl, whisk together Taste Flavor Co.
  2. Fiery Mango Habanero Sauce, olive oil, fresh lemon juice, chopped cilantro, sea salt, and pepper until well combined.
  3. Set aside.
  4. In a large bowl, heat butter until melted and stir in shrimp.
  5. Cook over medium heat, stirring for about 2 minutes or until seared.
  6. Transfer to a plate to cool.
  7. In a large bowl, mix together diced avocado, red onion, and cherry tomatoes.
  8. Stir in shrimp, and drizzle with the dressing.
  9. Serve right away.
