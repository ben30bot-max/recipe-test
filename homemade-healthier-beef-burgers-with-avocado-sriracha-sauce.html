Homemade Healthier Beef Burgers with Avocado-Sriracha Sauce

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

497 calories | 35.8g protein | 16.8g fat | 54.6g carbs

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1/3 cup grated carrot
  • 1/2 cup grated zucchini
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped red onion
  • 1/4 cup whole-wheat breadcrumbs
  • 4 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 teaspoon dried rosemary
  • 2 free-range eggs
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon sea salt
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 8 whole-wheat buns for serving

Directions

  1. In a blender, blend together avocado, Hot Honey Sriracha Sauce, and sea salt until well blended.
  2. Set aside.
  3. Combine beef mince, grated zucchini, grated carrot, parsley, red onion, breadcrumbs, garlic, rosemary, eggs, salt, and pepper in a large bowl; mix until well blended.
  4. Using your hands, roll small handfuls of the mixture into balls and then flatten into patties.
  5. Heat olive oil in a nonstick skillet and cook in the patties over medium-high heat for about 3 minutes per side or until cooked through.
  6. Place each patty onto of the four whole-grain buns.
  7. Drizzle each with the sauce and top with sliced tomato and lettuce, and then with each of the remaining buns.
  8. Serve right away!
