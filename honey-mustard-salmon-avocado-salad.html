Honey Mustard Salmon & Avocado Salad

Dinner · Yield: 4 servings

370 calories | 28g protein | 24g fat | 9g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 (4-ounce) salmon fillets, pan-seared
  • 4 cups baby spinach
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup toasted almonds
  • 2 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co.
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1/4 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

Directions

  1. Season salmon fillets with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and cook salmon for 4 minutes per side until golden and cooked through.
  3. Let cool slightly.
  4. In a small bowl, whisk together Honey Mustard Sauce, apple cider vinegar, olive oil, Dijon mustard, salt, and pepper.
  5. Spread baby spinach on plates.
  6. Top with salmon, avocado slices, cherry tomatoes, red bell pepper, and toasted almonds.
  7. Drizzle dressing over the salad.
  8. Toss lightly and serve immediately.
