Sesame Ginger Chicken Scramble

Breakfast · Yield: 2 Servings

245 calories | 20g protein | 16g fat | 11g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1/2 pound chicken
  • 4 free-range eggs
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 red onion, chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/4 cup diced red bell pepper
  • 4 tablespoons Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce
  • 2 tablespoons chopped chives
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame seeds

Directions

  1. Heat half of the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat, cook in the chicken until golden brown.
  2. Transfer to a plate.
  3. Heat the remaining oil in the same skillet and sauté red onions for about 4 minutes or until fragrant; stir in garlic for 1 minute.
  4. Add in the red bell pepper and cook for about 2 minutes.
  5. Return the chicken to the skillet and beat in the eggs.
  6. Cook, stirring, for about 2 minutes or until the eggs are set.
  7. Stir in the Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce and cook for 1 more minute.
  8. Garnish with chives and toasted sesame seeds before serving.
