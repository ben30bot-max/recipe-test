Hot Honey Sriracha Chicken Tacos

Dinner · Yield: 2 Tacos (1 Serving)

400 calories | 36g protein | 15g fat | 35g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 oz. grilled chicken breast, skinless, boneless
  • 2 small whole wheat tortillas
  • 1 tablespoon Hot Honey Sriracha sauce
  • 1/4 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1/4 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1/4 avocado (sliced)
  • 1 tablespoon Greek yogurt
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges

Directions

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
  2. Grill on medium heat for 5 minutes per side, until fully cooked.
  3. Let it rest for a few minutes, then slice into strips.
  4. Warm the tortillas in a skillet or microwave.
  5. Place grilled chicken strips in the center of each tortilla, drizzle with Hot Honey Sriracha sauce, and top with lettuce, tomatoes, and avocado slices.
  6. Add a dollop of Greek yogurt and a squeeze of lime juice over each taco.
  7. Serve immediately.
