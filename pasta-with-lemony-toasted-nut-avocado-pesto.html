Pasta with Lemony Toasted Nut & Avocado Pesto

Breakfast · Yield: 4 Servings

229 calories | 13.6g protein | 18.3g fat | 8.5g carbs

Ingredients

  • 8 oz pasta
  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves
  • 1/4 cup toasted cashews
  • 1/4 cup toasted almonds
  • 1 avocado
  • 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce

Directions

  1. Follow cooking instructions to cook pasta until tender.
  2. In a blender, combine basil leaves, pine nuts, garlic, avocado, olive oil, fresh lemon juice, sea salt, and pepper.
  3. Blend until very smooth.
  4. Toss the past with the pesto until evenly coated.
  5. Garnish with halved cherry tomatoes and serve as a light and nutritious starter to aid in weight loss.
