Thai-Inspired Chicken Burger with Mango Slaw

Dinner · Yield: 2 Burgers

345 calories | 28g protein | 10g fat | 35g carbs

Ingredients

  • For Patties:
  • 8 oz ground chicken breast
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped red bell pepper
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 clove garlic, minced For Mango Slaw:
  • 1/2 cup shredded green cabbage
  • 1/4 cup diced fresh mango
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh mint
  • 1 teaspoon fresh lime juice
  • 1 teaspoon Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce

Directions

  1. In a bowl, gently combine all patty ingredients.
  2. Form into two patties.
  3. In a separate bowl, toss together all the mango slaw ingredients.
  4. Set aside.
  5. Heat a skillet over medium heat.
  6. Cook chicken patties for 5 minutes per side, until cooked through.
  7. Brush the patties with 2 tablespoons of the Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce in the last minute of cooking.
  8. To assemble, place a patty on the bottom bun.
  9. Top with a generous pile of the mango slaw.
  10. Drizzle the remaining 1 tablespoon of sauce over the slaw, sprinkle with chopped peanuts, and add the top bun.
