5-Minute Turkey Breakfast Sandwiches

Breakfast · Yield: 1 serving

600 calories | 55g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 English muffins
  • 1 whole egg
  • 100g egg whites
  • 4 slices deli turkey
  • 1 slice light cheese
  • Hot sauce

Directions

  1. Scramble egg + whites.
  2. Toast the English muffins.
  3. Add turkey, cheese + hot sauce.
  4. Done.
