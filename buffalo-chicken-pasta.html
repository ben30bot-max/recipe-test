Buffalo Chicken Pasta

Dinner · Yield: 1 serving

620 calories | 70g protein

Ingredients

  • 65g penne pasta, dry
  • 140g cooked, shredded chicken breast
  • ¼ cup (60ml) buffalo sauce
  • ½ cup (120g) 0% Greek yogurt
  • 20g shredded light cheese
  • Green onions, optional

Directions

  1. Cook the pasta according to package directions.
  2. Season and cook the chicken (or use rotisserie, shredded).
  3. Mix pasta, chicken, buffalo sauce and Greek yogurt in a bowl.
  4. Stir in the shredded cheese.
  5. Top with green onions and enjoy.
