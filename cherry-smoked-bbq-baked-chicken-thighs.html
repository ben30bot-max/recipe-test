Cherry Smoked BBQ Baked Chicken Thighs

Dinner · Yield: 1 Serving

410 calories | 32g protein | 15g fat | 35g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 oz. boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 2 tablespoons Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce
  • 1/2 cup sweet potatoes, diced
  • 1/2 cup steamed broccoli
  • 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • ¼ teaspoon sea salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Season the chicken thighs with salt and pepper.
  3. Place them in a baking dish and coat them with the Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce.
  4. Add the sweet potatoes to the baking dish, drizzle with olive oil, and roast in the oven for 30 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the sweet potatoes are tender.
  5. Plate the chicken thighs with sweet potatoes and steamed broccoli on the side.
