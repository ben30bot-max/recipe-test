Grilled Mango Habanero Steak with Rocket & Pistachio Herb Sauce

Dinner · Yield: 4 servings

590 calories | 36g protein | 45g fat | 6g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 lean beef steaks
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 cup baby spinach
  • 1 cup rocket (arugula)
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 cup Taste Flavor Co. Mango Habanero Sauce For Pistachio Herb Sauce:
  • 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/3 cup toasted pistachios
  • 1/3 cup toasted almonds
  • 1 garlic clove, minced

Directions

  1. In a food processor, pulse pistachios, almonds, garlic, basil, mint, rocket, and lemon juice.
  2. Slowly drizzle in olive oil and blend until creamy.
  3. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Preheat a grill or grill pan over medium heat.
  5. Rub steaks with olive oil, season with salt and pepper, and grill for about 4 minutes per side or until cooked to your liking.
  6. Let steaks rest for a few minutes, then slice.
  7. Arrange spinach, rocket, and cherry tomatoes on serving plates.
  8. Top with sliced steak and drizzle with mango habanero sauce and pistachio herb sauce.
  9. Serve warm with optional lemon wedges.
