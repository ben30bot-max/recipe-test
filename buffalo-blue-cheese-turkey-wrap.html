Buffalo Blue Cheese Turkey Wrap

Dinner · Yield: 4 wraps

290 calories | 22g protein | 16g fat | 23g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 large whole-wheat tortillas
  • 8 slices lean deli turkey
  • 4 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co.
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1/2 avocado, sliced
  • 1/4 cup crumbled blue cheese

Directions

  1. Lightly warm the tortillas in a skillet or microwave.
  2. Spread 2 tablespoons of Buffalo Blue Cheese Sauce on each tortilla.
  3. Add 2 slices of turkey, shredded lettuce, avocado slices, and crumbled blue cheese.
  4. Fold in the sides and roll tightly into a wrap.
  5. Cut in half and serve.
