Buffalo Blue Cheese Chicken Wings with Healthy Greek Yogurt Herb Dipping Sauce

Dinner · Yield: 4 servings

445 calories | 27g protein | 30g fat | 12g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds chicken wings
  • 1/2 cup Taste Flavor Co. Buffalo Blue Cheese Sauce
  • 1/4 cup butter, melted
  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt (non-fat or full-fat based
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 small garlic clove, minced
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon fresh dill, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon smoked paprika

Directions

  1. Prepare the dipping sauce by stirring together Greek yogurt, lemon juice, and olive oil in a bowl.
  2. Stir in minced garlic, parsley, dill, onion powder, sea salt, black pepper, and smoked paprika.
  3. Mix well to incorporate all flavors and transfer the sauce to a serving bowl.
  4. Let it chill in the fridge for at least 10 minutes to allow the flavors to meld.
  5. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  6. Line a baking sheet with foil for easy cleanup.
  7. In a large bowl, toss the chicken wings with melted butter to coat evenly.
  8. Arrange the wings on the prepared baking sheet in a single layer.
  9. Bake for 40 minutes, flipping halfway through, until golden and crispy.
  10. Once cooked, transfer the wings to a clean bowl.
  11. Add the Taste Flavor Co.
  12. Buffalo Blue Cheese Sauce and toss until evenly coated.
  13. Serve immediately with the Greek yogurt herb dipping sauce on the side.
