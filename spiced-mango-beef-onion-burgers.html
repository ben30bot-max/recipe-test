Spiced Mango Beef & Onion Burgers

Dinner · Yield: 4 Burgers

410 calories | 35g protein | 18g fat | 35g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 2 free-range eggs
  • 1/2 cup gluten-free breadcrumbs
  • ¼ teaspoon sea salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 cup finely chopped red onion
  • 4 whole wheat buns
  • Lettuce, tomato, and onion slices
  • 4 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co.

Directions

  1. Mix beef, egg, breadcrumbs, chopped onions, sea salt, and pepper.
  2. Form 4 patties from the mixture.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan and sear the patties for about 6 minutes per side or until cooked through and golden brown on the outside.
  4. Assemble on buns with veggies and the cooked patties.
  5. Drizzle each with a generous amount of the sauce and serve right away.
