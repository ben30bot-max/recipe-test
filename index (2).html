Hot Honey Sriracha Shrimp Stir-Fry Pasta

Dinner · Yield: 1 serving · Total time: 30 minutes

450 calories | 33g protein | 15g fat | 50g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 oz. whole wheat pasta
  • 4 oz. shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1 tablespoon Hot Honey Sriracha sauce
  • 1/2 cup snap peas
  • 1/2 cup carrots, julienned
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 teaspoon sesame seeds

Directions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
  2. Cook the whole wheat pasta according to package instructions (10 minutes).
  3. Drain and set aside.
  4. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  5. Add the shrimp and garlic, cooking for 3 minutes on each side until shrimp are pink and cooked through.
  6. Remove from the skillet and set aside.
  7. In the same skillet, stir-fry the snap peas and carrots for 4 minutes until tender but crisp.
  8. Add the cooked pasta and shrimp back to the skillet.
  9. Toss everything with the Hot Honey Sriracha sauce and sesame seeds.
  10. Serve immediately.
