Spiced Buffalo Blue Chicken Burger Bowl (Bun-Free)

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

345 calories | 29g protein | 23g fat | 13g carbs

Ingredients

  • 300g ground chicken (lean, 93% lean/7% fat)
  • 1 tablespoon almond flour (or breadcrumbs)
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 2 cups mixed greens (spinach, arugula, and romaine)
  • 1 medium carrot, shredded
  • ½ cucumber, sliced
  • 1 medium avocado, diced
  • 1 tablespoon chopped green onions
  • 4 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co.
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice

Directions

  1. In a mixing bowl, combine ground chicken, almond flour, garlic powder, salt, and cayenne pepper.
  2. Form into 2 patties.
  3. Heat a non-stick skillet or grill pan over medium heat.
  4. Cook the patties for 6 minutes per side or until well cooked.
  5. Assemble the bowl: • Divide the mixed greens, carrot, cucumber, and avocado between two bowls. • Top each bowl with a cooked chicken patty.
  6. Mix the Buffalo Blue Cheese Sauce with lemon juice and drizzle over the bowl.
  7. Garnish with chopped green onions and enjoy!
