Cheesy Vegetable Omelet Wraps

Breakfast · Yield: 2 Servings

290 calories | 18g protein | 15g fat | 23g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs, beaten
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 1 eggplant, diced
  • 1/2 cup diced bell peppers (mixed colors)
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheese
  • 2 tablespoons Southwest Ranch Sauce
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 whole-wheat wraps

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil and sauté red onion until soft.
  2. Add in red bell peppers and eggplant and cook for about 4 minutes or until soft.
  3. Pour in eggs, cook, and fold into a fluffy omelet.
  4. Slice the omelet in half, place each portion on a wrap, and top with cheese.
  5. Drizzle with Southwest Ranch Sauce, roll up, and serve.
