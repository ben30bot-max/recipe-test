Lazy Beef Taco Bowl

Lunch · Yield: 1 serving

750 calories | 75g protein

Ingredients

  • 8 oz (227g) 96% lean ground beef
  • 1 cup cooked jasmine rice
  • ½ cup black beans
  • 15g shredded light cheese
  • 100g 0% Greek yogurt
  • Salsa
  • Shredded lettuce
  • Taco seasoning

Directions

  1. Brown the beef with taco seasoning.
  2. Put everything in a bowl.
  3. That's genuinely it.
