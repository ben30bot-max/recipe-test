Crispy Chicken Parmesan Wrap

Lunch · Yield: 1 serving

670 calories | 60g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 large flour tortilla
  • 6 oz cooked chicken breast
  • 1 oz mozzarella
  • 2 tbsp marinara
  • 10g Parmesan
  • 1 tsp olive oil

Directions

  1. Season the chicken.
  2. Crisp it in a pan or air fryer.
  3. Wrap everything together.
