Southwest Ranch Steak & Sweet Potato Fries

Dinner · Yield: 2 Servings

425 calories | 30g protein | 18g fat | 35g carbs

Ingredients

  • 8 oz lean sirloin steak
  • 1 large sweet potato, cut into fries
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 cups ready-made coleslaw mix (shredded cabbage & carrots)
  • 2 tablespoons Southwest Ranch Sauce
  • 1 teaspoon freshly squeezed lime juice

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Toss sweet potato fries with half of the olive oil and smoked paprika.
  3. Spread on a baking sheet and bake for 25 minutes, flipping halfway, until crispy.
  4. Season steak with salt and pepper.
  5. Heat remaining olive oil in a cast-iron skillet over high heat.
  6. Cook steak for 5 minutes per side for medium-rare.
  7. Let rest for 5 minutes before slicing.
  8. In a small bowl, toss the coleslaw mix with Southwest Ranch Sauce and lime juice.
  9. Serve the sliced steak with sweet potato fries and the creamy slaw on the side.
