Honey Mustard Beef & Avocado Wrap

Dinner · Yield: 4 wraps

480 calories | 30g protein | 28g fat | 28g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 4 spinach tortillas
  • 1/2 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup shredded romaine lettuce

Directions

  1. Brown the ground beef in a skillet over medium heat, seasoning with salt and pepper.
  2. Drain excess fat.
  3. Mix Honey Mustard Sauce and Greek yogurt in a small bowl.
  4. Spread the sauce over the spinach tortillas.
  5. Add shredded romaine, ground beef, avocado slices, diced tomatoes, and shredded cheddar cheese.
  6. Roll tightly and enjoy immediately.
