Honey Mustard Salmon Bowl

Dinner · Yield: 2 servings

380 calories | 28g protein | 20g fat | 28g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets (4 oz each)
  • 2 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co.
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • 1 cup cooked brown rice
  • 1 cup steamed broccoli
  • 1/2 avocado, sliced

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Place salmon on a lined baking sheet and brush with Honey Mustard Sauce.
  3. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until salmon flakes easily with a fork.
  4. Serve salmon over brown rice with broccoli and avocado slices.
  5. Drizzle with extra Honey Mustard Sauce if desired.
