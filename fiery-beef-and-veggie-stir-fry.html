Fiery Beef and Veggie Stir-Fry

Lunch · Yield: 4 Servings

290 calories | 35g protein | 13g fat | 10g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean flank steak, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 2 red onions, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 4 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co. Fiery Mango Habanero Sauce

Directions

  1. Heat oil in a wok or skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Add beef and cook for 5 minutes or until golden brown.
  3. Add veggies and stir-fry for another 7 minutes until tender-crisp.
  4. Drizzle in sauce and stir to coat evenly.
  5. Serve hot.
