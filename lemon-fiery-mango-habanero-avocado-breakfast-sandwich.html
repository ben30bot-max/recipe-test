Lemon & Fiery Mango Habanero Avocado Breakfast Sandwich

Breakfast · Yield: 2 Servings

487 calories | 16g protein | 28g fat | 46g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 slices whole grain bread, toasted
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 ripe avocado, mashed
  • 1 red onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 2 free-range eggs
  • 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co. Fiery Mango Habanero Sauce
  • ¼ teaspoon sea salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper

Directions

  1. In a small bowl, whisk together the Taste Flavor Co.
  2. Fiery Mango Habanero Sauce, fresh lemon juice, sea salt and pepper until well combined.
  3. Heat olive oil in a skillet and fry the eggs.
  4. Toast the bread until golden.
  5. Spread mashed avocado on each slice.
  6. Place a fried egg on two slices of toast, then layer with tomato and onion slices.
  7. Top each with the remaining slices of toast to form sandwiches.
  8. Drizzle with 1 tablespoon of lemon-mango habanero sauce and serve.
