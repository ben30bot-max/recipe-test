Sesame Ginger Steak & Broccoli Pizza

Dinner · Yield: 2 Servings

345 calories | 25g protein | 11g fat | 35g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 pre-baked whole wheat thin pizza crust (10-inch)
  • 4 oz flank steak, very thinly sliced
  • 1 cup small broccoli florets, blanched
  • 1/4 cup thinly sliced red onion
  • 1/4 cup part-skim mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • 3 tablespoons Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce
  • 1 teaspoon sesame seeds
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh cilantro

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Spread 2 tablespoons of the Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce evenly over the pizza crust.
  3. Top with the sliced steak, broccoli florets, and red onion.
  4. Sprinkle with mozzarella cheese.
  5. Bake for 15 minutes until the crust is crispy and the cheese is bubbly.
  6. Drizzle with the remaining 1 tbsp of sauce and garnish with sesame seeds and cilantro before slicing.
