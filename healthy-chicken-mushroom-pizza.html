Healthy Chicken & Mushroom Pizza

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

361 calories | 30g protein | 13g fat | 32g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 whole wheat pizza crust (10-inch)
  • 1 cup chicken breast, diced
  • 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup bell peppers, diced
  • 1 cup diced mushroom
  • 4 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co. Fiery Mango Habanero Sauce

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Place crust on baking tray.
  3. Heat olive oil in a skillet and cook chicken until golden brown.
  4. Spread sauce over crust and top with chicken, mushroom, onion, peppers, and cheese.
  5. Bake for 15 minutes until cheese is bubbly and crust is golden.
