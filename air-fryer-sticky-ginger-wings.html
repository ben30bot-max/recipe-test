Air Fryer Sticky Ginger Wings

Dinner · Yield: 2 Servings

295 calories | 30g protein | 16g fat | 7g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken wings
  • 1/4 cup Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce
  • 1 teaspoon sriracha
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame seeds
  • 1 tablespoon chopped cilantro

Directions

  1. Pat chicken wings dry.
  2. Place in air fryer basket in a single layer.
  3. Air fry at 400°F for 20 minutes, shaking the basket halfway through, until wings are crispy and cooked through.
  4. In a large bowl, whisk together the Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce and sriracha.
  5. Toss the hot wings in the sauce until evenly glazed.
  6. Garnish with sesame seeds and cilantro before serving.
