Spicy Mango BBQ Beef & Caramelized Onion Pizza

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

350 calories | 27g protein | 14g fat | 28g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 pre-baked whole wheat or gluten-free pizza crust (10–12 inches)
  • 3/4 cup cooked shredded or ground beef
  • 1 cup red onion, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/3 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup Taste Flavor Co. Mango Habanero Sauce (plus more for drizzle)
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Heat olive oil in a skillet and cook onion over low heat for about 12 minutes until caramelized.
  3. Stir in balsamic vinegar and cook 1 more minute.
  4. Spread mango habanero sauce over the pizza crust.
  5. Add shredded beef and caramelized onions.
  6. Sprinkle with mozzarella and bake for about 20 minutes until the cheese is melted and crust is golden.
  7. Drizzle with extra sauce and top with fresh basil before serving.
