Satisfying Chicken Burger with Lemon-Mango Habanero & Peanut Sauce

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

381 calories | 35g protein | 15g fat | 27g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground chicken breast
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped red onion
  • 1/4 cup breadcrumbs (or almond flour for low carb)
  • 2 free-range eggs
  • 1 tablespoon chopped parsley
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • ¼ teaspoon sea salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • 4 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co. Mango Habanero Sauce
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons peanut butter

Directions

  1. In a small bowl, whisk together the Taste Flavor Co.
  2. Mango Habanero Sauce, lemon juice, and peanut butter until well blended; set aside.
  3. In a bowl, combine ground chicken, onion, breadcrumbs, egg, parsley, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  4. Form into 4 equal patties.
  5. Grill or pan-fry patties over medium heat for 7 minutes per side, or until cooked through.
  6. Toast burger buns and spread with habanero-peanutlemon sauce.
  7. Assemble with lettuce, tomato, and chicken patty.
  8. Serve immediately.
