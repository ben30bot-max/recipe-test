MACRO-FRIENDLY APPETIZERS Hot Honey Sriracha Sweet Potato Wedges

Dinner · Yield: 1 Serving

180 calories | 2g protein | 6g fat | 28g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 medium sweet potato, cut into wedges
  • 1 tablespoon Hot Honey Sriracha sauce
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. In a bowl, toss sweet potato wedges with olive oil, Hot Honey Sriracha sauce, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Spread the wedges in a single layer on a baking sheet.
  4. Bake for 30 minutes, flipping halfway through, until crispy and golden brown.
  5. Serve warm.
