Honey Garlic Beef Noodles

Dinner · Yield: 1 serving

665 calories | 60g protein

Ingredients

  • 170g lean ground beef (96%), cooked
  • 65g noodles (lo mein or spaghetti), dry
  • 150g stir-fry vegetables
  • 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 tsp garlic powder)
  • Green onions, optional

Directions

  1. Cook the noodles according to package directions.
  2. While the noodles cook, brown the beef. Season with salt, pepper, garlic.
  3. Add stir-fry vegetables and cook until tender.
  4. Stir in soy sauce and honey; simmer 1–2 minutes.
  5. Toss in the noodles and mix until coated. Top with green onions.
