Honey Sriracha Avocado Toast

Breakfast · Yield: 4 Servings

241 calories | 9.6g protein | 9.1g fat | 27.3g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 slices whole grain bread, toasted
  • 1 ripe avocado, mashed
  • 2 tablespoons Hot Honey Sriracha sauce
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice
  • 1/4 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, sliced into halves
  • Cherry tomatoes, sliced

Directions

  1. In a small bowl, mix together the mashed avocado, Hot Honey Sriracha sauce, lime juice, salt, and pepper.
  2. Spread the avocado mixture evenly onto the toasted whole grain bread slices.
  3. Top each avocado toast with half of the hard boiled eggs and sliced cherry tomatoes.
  4. Serve immediately for a delicious and satisfying breakfast option.
