MACRO-FRIENDLY APPETIZERS Mango & Avocado Salad with Citrus-Honey Sriracha Dressing

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

358 calories | 3.3g protein | 17.6g fat | 46.4g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe mangoes, peeled and diced
  • 2 ripe avocados, diced
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1/4 cup red onion, finely chopped
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons fresh orange juice

Directions

  1. In a large bowl, combine diced mangoes, diced avocados, diced cucumber, chopped red onion, and chopped cilantro.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk together fresh orange juice, fresh lime juice, Hot Honey Sriracha sauce, olive oil, salt, and pepper to make the dressing.
  3. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss gently to coat all ingredients.
  4. Serve chilled as a vibrant and flavorful starter.
