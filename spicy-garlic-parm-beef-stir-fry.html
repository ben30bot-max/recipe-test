Spicy Garlic Parm Beef Stir-Fry

Dinner · Yield: 2 Servings

355 calories | 41g protein | 16g fat | 11g carbs

Ingredients

  • 8 oz. lean beef strips (sirloin or flank)
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 small zucchini, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons Spicy Garlic Parm sauce
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame seeds

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Add beef strips and stir-fry until browned and cooked through (about 10 minutes).
  3. Add broccoli, bell pepper, and zucchini, stir-fry for another 5 minutes until vegetables are tender but crisp.
  4. Pour in Spicy Garlic Parm sauce, tossing everything together until well-coated.
  5. Serve topped with sesame seeds if desired.
