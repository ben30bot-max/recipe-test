Cherry Smoked BBQ Veggie Scramble

Breakfast · Yield: 1 Serving

250 calories | 15g protein | 14g fat | 15g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 egg whites
  • 1 whole egg
  • 1/2 cup diced zucchini
  • 1/4 cup diced onions
  • 1/4 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add zucchini, onions, and cherry tomatoes.
  3. Sauté for 5 minutes or until tender.
  4. In a bowl, whisk egg whites and whole egg.
  5. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Pour the egg mixture into the skillet and cook, stirring gently, for 5 minutes or until eggs are cooked through.
  7. Drizzle Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce over the scramble and serve.
