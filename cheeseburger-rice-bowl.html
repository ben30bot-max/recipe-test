Cheeseburger Rice Bowl

Dinner · Yield: 1 serving

710 calories | 60g protein

Ingredients

  • 170g lean ground beef (96%), cooked
  • 1½ cups (240g) cooked jasmine rice
  • 15g shredded light cheese
  • 70g lettuce
  • 40g pickles
  • 30g light burger sauce

Directions

  1. Cook the ground beef with salt, pepper, garlic powder.
  2. Cook the rice according to package directions.
  3. Layer rice, beef, lettuce, pickles and cheese in a bowl.
  4. Drizzle with burger sauce.
  5. Mix it all together and enjoy.
