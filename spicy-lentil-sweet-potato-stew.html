Spicy Lentil & Sweet Potato Stew

Lunch · Yield: 4 Servings

260 calories | 12g protein | 6g fat | 34g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 cup diced sweet potato
  • 1/2 cup red lentils
  • 2 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 1/4 cup Taste Flavor Co. Mango Habanero Sauce
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • ¼ teaspoon sea salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a pot over medium heat.
  2. Sauté onion and garlic until translucent.
  3. Add sweet potato, lentils, broth, mango habanero sauce, cumin, salt, and pepper.
  4. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer and cook for about 30 minutes until lentils and sweet potatoes are soft.
  5. Adjust seasoning and serve hot.
