Lemon-Pepper Chicken Wings

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

397 calories | 52.7g protein | 14.3g fat | 16.1g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds chicken wings
  • 1/2 cup Hot Honey Sriracha sauce
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon zest
  • 4 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons avocado oil
  • 1 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. In a bowl, mix Hot Honey Sriracha sauce, lemon zest, fresh lemon juice, avocado oil, sea salt, and pepper.
  3. Toss chicken wings in the sauce until well coated.
  4. Place wings on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper and bake for 45-50 minutes or until crispy.
  5. Serve with lemon wedges on the side.
