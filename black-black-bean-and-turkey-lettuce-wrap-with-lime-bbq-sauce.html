Black Black Bean and Turkey Lettuce Wrap with Lime-BBQ sauce

Dinner · Yield: 8 Servings

357 calories | 27.6g protein | 10.8g fat | 42.1g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground turkey
  • 2 cups canned black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1/2 cup whole-grain bread crumbs
  • 1/2 cup diced onion
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • ½ teaspoon sea salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 red onions, sliced into rings
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced, for serving
  • 2 tablespoons Cherry Smoked BBQ Sauce
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice
  • 8 lettuce leaves

Directions

  1. In small bowl, mix together Cherry Smoked BBQ Sauce, fresh lime juice, and a pinch of salt; set aside.
  2. Heat olive oil in a skillet and fry red onion rings until caramelized.
  3. Transfer to a plate and set aside.
  4. In a food processor, pulse the black beans until roughly mashed.
  5. In a mixing bowl, combine the ground turkey, mashed black beans, breadcrumbs, diced onion, minced garlic, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
  6. Mix well.
  7. Form the mixture into burger patties.
  8. Preheat a grill or skillet over medium heat.
  9. Cook the burgers for about 6 minutes per side, or until fully cooked through.
  10. Assemble the burgers: Place the cooked patties on the lettuce leaves and top with sliced tomato, and red onion.
  11. Drizzle cherry lime-BBQ sauce on each and wrap to serve.
