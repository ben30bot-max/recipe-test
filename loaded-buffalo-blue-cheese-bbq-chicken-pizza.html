Loaded Buffalo Blue Cheese BBQ Chicken Pizza

Dinner · Yield: 4 servings

380 calories | 32g protein | 18g fat | 32g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 thin whole-wheat pizza crust
  • 4 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co.
  • 1/4 cup low-sugar BBQ sauce
  • 1 cup shredded cooked chicken breast
  • 1/2 cup thinly sliced bell peppers
  • 1/2 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 1/4 cup thinly sliced red onion
  • 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella cheese (low-fat)
  • 1/4 cup crumbled blue cheese
  • 1/4 cup sliced green onions
  • 1/4 tsp crushed red pepper flakes

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Mix Taste Flavor Co.
  3. Buffalo Blue Cheese Sauce with BBQ sauce and spread evenly over the pizza crust.
  4. Top with shredded chicken, mushrooms, red onion, bell peppers, mozzarella, and crumbled blue cheese.
  5. Bake for 15-20 minutes, or until the crust is crispy and the cheese is melted.
  6. Garnish with green onions and serve.
