Healthy Pancakes with Lemon Honey Mango Habanero Drizzle

Breakfast · Yield: 3 servings

514 calories | 14g protein | 33g fat | 46g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup coconut flour
  • 1 cup blanched almond flour
  • 1/3 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1/4 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1 cup fresh blueberries, for serving For the Drizzle:

Directions

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together coconut flour, almond flour, almond milk, eggs, oil, baking powder, vanilla, and salt until smooth.
  2. Heat a skillet over medium heat and lightly grease with olive oil.
  3. Drop about 3 tablespoons of batter into the pan for each pancake and cook for about 2 minutes per side, or until lightly browned.
  4. In a small bowl, whisk together honey, lemon juice, and mango habanero sauce to make the drizzle.
  5. Serve pancakes topped with fresh blueberries and a generous drizzle of the sweet and spicy honey-lemon sauce.
