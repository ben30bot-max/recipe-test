Honey Mustard Grilled Chicken Breast

Dinner · Yield: 4 servings

200 calories | 30g protein | 7g fat | 5g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 3 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co.
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon sea salt

Directions

  1. Combine Honey Mustard Sauce, olive oil, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
  2. Coat the chicken breasts in the marinade and let them rest for 15 minutes.
  3. Preheat a grill or grill pan over medium heat.
  4. Cook chicken for 6-8 minutes per side or until internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).
  5. Serve with roasted vegetables or a quinoa salad.
