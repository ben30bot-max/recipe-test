MACRO-FRIENDLY APPETIZERS Lime Garlic Parm Shrimp Skewers

Dinner · Yield: 3 Servings

219 calories | 33.2g protein | 0.5g fat | 18.2g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 pound large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 2 tablespoons Spicy Garlic Parm sauce
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice
  • 1 tablespoon raw honey
  • 1 teaspoon minced garlic
  • 1/4 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • Wooden skewers, soaked in water for 30 minutes
  • Chopped fresh cilantro for garnish
  • Lime wedges for serving

Directions

  1. In a small bowl, whisk together the Spicy Garlic Parm sauce, lime juice, honey, minced garlic, salt, and pepper.
  2. Thread the shrimp onto the soaked wooden skewers, leaving a little space between each shrimp.
  3. Brush the Honey Sriracha sauce mixture over the shrimp skewers, coating them evenly.
  4. Preheat a grill or grill pan over medium-high heat.
  5. Grill the shrimp skewers for 5 minutes on each side, or until the shrimp are cooked through and opaque.
  6. Remove the shrimp skewers from the grill and transfer to a serving platter.
  7. Garnish with chopped fresh cilantro and serve with lime wedges on the side for squeezing over the shrimp.
