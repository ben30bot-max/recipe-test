Green Onion Lemon- Pepper Chicken Wings

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

490 calories | 66g protein | 20.5g fat | 3.6g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds chicken wings, split at joints
  • 2 tablespoons Spicy Garlic Parm sauce
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon zest
  • 4 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • 1/2 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 cup chopped fresh green onions

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. In a bowl, mix Spicy Garlic Parm sauce, olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, salt, and red pepper flakes.
  3. Toss chicken wings in the sauce mixture until well coated.
  4. Place wings on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper and bake for 45-50 minutes or until crispy.
  5. Garnish with chopped fresh green onions before serving.
