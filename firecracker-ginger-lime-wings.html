Firecracker Ginger Lime Wings

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

315 calories | 29g protein | 18g fat | 8g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs chicken wings
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 1/3 cup Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce
  • Zest and juice of 1 lime
  • 2 tablespoons chopped green onion

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Toss wings with olive oil, salt, and cayenne pepper.
  3. Arrange on a wire rack set over a baking sheet.
  4. Bake for 40 minutes until crispy and cooked through.
  5. In a bowl, combine the Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce, lime zest, and lime juice.
  6. Toss the hot wings in the sauce until fully coated.
  7. Garnish with chopped green onion and serve immediately.
