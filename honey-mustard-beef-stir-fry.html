Honey Mustard Beef Stir-Fry

Dinner · Yield: 4 servings

350 calories | 30g protein | 22g fat | 12g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 pound flank steak, thinly sliced
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 1 zucchini, sliced
  • 1/2 onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup snap peas
  • 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, minced
  • 1/4 cup Taste Flavor Co.
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar

Directions

  1. In a small bowl, mix together the Honey Mustard Sauce, soy sauce, and rice vinegar.
  2. Set aside.
  3. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat.
  4. Add the thinly sliced flank steak and stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until browned and cooked through.
  5. Remove from the skillet and set aside.
  6. In the same skillet, add the red bell pepper, zucchini, onion, snap peas, and ginger.
  7. Stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until the vegetables are tender-crisp.
  8. Return the beef to the skillet and pour in the Honey Mustard sauce.
  9. Stir to coat everything evenly and cook for another 1-2 minutes until heated through.
  10. Serve the stir-fry over brown rice or quinoa for a complete meal.
