MACRO-FRIENDLY FRIES Lime BBQ Jicama Fries

Dinner · Yield: 2 Servings

175 calories | 2.8g protein | 0.4g fat | 37.7g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 medium jicama, peeled and cut into matchsticks
  • 3 tablespoons Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce
  • 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lime juice
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley

Directions

  1. In a bowl, toss jicama matchsticks with Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce, lime juice, sea salt, and smoked paprika.
  2. Spread on a plate and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
  3. Serve chilled, garnished with fresh parsley.
