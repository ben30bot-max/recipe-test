Crispy Baked Southwest Ranch Chicken Wings

Dinner · Yield: 2 Servings

385 calories | 38g protein | 22g fat | 7g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs chicken wingettes and drumettes
  • 1/2 cup Southwest Ranch Sauce
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon chopped cilantro for garnish
  • Extra Southwest Ranch Sauce for dipping

Directions

  1. In a large bowl, toss the chicken wings with Southwest Ranch Sauce, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  2. Cover and marinate in the fridge for at least 30 minutes (or up to 4 hours).
  3. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  4. Place a wire rack on a baking sheet and coat with non-stick spray.
  5. Arrange the wings in a single layer on the rack.
  6. Bake for 35-40 minutes, flipping halfway, until crispy and cooked through (internal temperature of 165°F).
  7. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve with extra Southwest Ranch Sauce for dipping.
