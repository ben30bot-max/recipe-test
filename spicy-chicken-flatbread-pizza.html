Spicy Chicken Flatbread Pizza

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

330 calories | 30g protein | 11g fat | 24g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 whole grain flatbreads
  • 1 cup diced chicken
  • 1/4 cup mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup diced red onion
  • 1 cup diced cherry tomato
  • 1 cup diced red and yellow bell peppers
  • 1/4 cup chopped spinach
  • 4 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co. Fiery Mango Habanero Sauce

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a skillet and cook chicken until golden brown.
  2. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  3. Layer cooked chicken, red onion, bell pepper, tomato, and spinach on flatbread.
  4. Sprinkle with cheese and bake for 10–12 minutes.
  5. Drizzle with Taste Flavor Co.
  6. Fiery Mango Habanero Sauce before serving.
