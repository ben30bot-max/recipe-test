Sesame Ginger Veggie & Egg Scramble

Breakfast · Yield: 2 Servings

240 calories | 16g protein | 17g fat | 7g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 large eggs, whisked
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 cup chopped mushrooms
  • 1/4 cup diced red bell pepper
  • 2 tablespoons chopped green onion
  • 1 cup fresh spinach
  • 2 tablespoons Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame seeds

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a non-stick skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add mushrooms and bell pepper, sauté for 4 minutes until softened.
  3. Add spinach and cook until just wilted.
  4. Pour in the whisked eggs.
  5. Gently scramble until eggs are cooked to your liking.
  6. Stir in the Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce.
  7. Garnish with green onion and sesame seeds before serving.
