Fiery Mango Habanero Breakfast Wrap

Breakfast · Yield: 4 Wraps

326 calories | 14g protein | 21g fat | 27g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 large whole-wheat tortillas
  • 4 free-range eggs (or egg whites for lower fat)
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 cup diced red bell pepper
  • 1/2 cup diced green onion
  • 2 avocados, sliced
  • 4 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co. Fiery Mango Habanero Sauce
  • 1/4 cup shredded low-fat cheddar cheese
  • ¼ teaspoon sea salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a skillet and scramble the eggs and red bell peppers together in a skillet until fully cooked.
  2. Lightly warm the tortillas.
  3. Layer eggs, avocado slices, cheddar cheese, and green onion on the tortillas.
  4. Drizzle 1 tablespoon of Fiery Mango Habanero Sauce over each.
  5. Roll into wraps and serve warm.
