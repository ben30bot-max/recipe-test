MACRO-FRIENDLY FRIES Sriracha-Flavored Russet Potato Fries

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

220 calories | 4g protein | 3.8g fat | 48.4g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds russet potatoes, cut into fries
  • 3 tablespoons Hot Honey Sriracha sauce
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. In a large bowl, toss russet potato fries with Hot Honey Sriracha sauce, olive oil, garlic powder, and onion powder until evenly coated.
  3. Spread the fries on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper, ensuring they are in a single layer for even cooking.
  4. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until the fries are golden brown and crispy, flipping them halfway through.
  5. Sprinkle with sea salt and chopped fresh parsley before serving.
