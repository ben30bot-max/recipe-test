Honey Mustard Grilled Lean Steak Salad

Dinner · Yield: 4 servings

375 calories | 35g protein | 22g fat | 16g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 cups mixed salad greens
  • 1 pound lean steak
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup shredded carrots
  • 1/4 cup crumbled feta cheese
  • 2 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co.
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon fresh lemon juice
  • 1/4 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

Directions

  1. Season steak with salt and pepper.
  2. Grill over medium heat for 10minutes per side until fully cooked.
  3. Slice thinly.
  4. In a small bowl, whisk together Honey Mustard Sauce, olive oil, fresh lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  5. Arrange salad greens on a platter.
  6. Top with grilled steak slices, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, shredded carrots, and feta cheese.
  7. Drizzle dressing over the salad and toss gently before serving.
