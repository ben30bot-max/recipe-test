Honey Mustard Beef & Veggie Wrap

Dinner · Yield: 4 wraps

350 calories | 25g protein | 14g fat | 30g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 grilled chicken breasts, sliced
  • 4 large whole-grain tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1/2 cup shredded carrots
  • 1/2 cucumber, julienned
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced

Directions

  1. In a small bowl, mix Honey Mustard Sauce, Greek yogurt, and lemon juice.
  2. Spread the honey mustard sauce over each tortilla.
  3. Layer shredded lettuce, carrots, cucumber, red onion, grilled chicken slices, and feta cheese.
  4. Roll the tortillas tightly, slice in half if desired, and serve immediately.
