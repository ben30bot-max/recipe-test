MACRO-FRIENDLY APPETIZERS Caramelized Onion Turkey Lettuce Wraps

Dinner · Yield: 4 servings

380 calories | 33.2g protein | 19.7g fat | 18.9g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground turkey
  • 1/3 cup Spicy Garlic-Parm sauce
  • 1 tablespoon low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
  • 1 cup shredded carrots
  • 1 cup shredded red cabbage
  • 1/2 cup cucumber, julienned
  • 4 red onions, sliced
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 8 butter lettuce leaves

Directions

  1. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat.
  2. Add the sliced red onions and fry until caramelized.
  3. Transfer the onions to a plate and set aside.
  4. Add ground turkey to the skillet and cook until browned, breaking it apart with a spoon as it cooks.
  5. Add minced garlic and grated ginger to the turkey, sautéing for an additional 2 minutes until fragrant.
  6. Pour in the Spicy Garlic-Parm sauce and low-sodium soy sauce, stirring to coat the turkey evenly.
  7. Simmer for 5-7 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  8. While the turkey is simmering, prepare the shredded carrots, red cabbage, and julienned cucumber.
  9. Assemble the lettuce wraps by spooning the hot honey sriracha turkey mixture into each lettuce leaf.
  10. Top with shredded carrots, red cabbage, cucumber, caramelized red onions, and fresh cilantro.
  11. Serve immediately and enjoy these wraps as a flavorful and low-calorie meal.
