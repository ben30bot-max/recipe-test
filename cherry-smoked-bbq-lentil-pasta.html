Cherry Smoked BBQ Lentil Pasta

Dinner · Yield: 1 Serving

380 calories | 22g protein | 24g fat | 54g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 oz. lentil pasta
  • 2 tablespoons Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce
  • 1 cup diced red onion
  • 1/2 cup roasted cherry tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup corn kernels, grilled
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil

Directions

  1. Bring a pot of water to a boil.
  2. Cook the lentil pasta for 10 minutes until tender but still firm.
  3. Drain and set aside.
  4. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
  5. Add the diced red onion and cook for 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until caramelized and softened.
  6. Preheat your oven to 400°F.
  7. Place the cherry tomatoes and corn on a baking sheet, drizzle with olive oil, and roast for 12 minutes until they begin to blister and brown slightly.
  8. In a large bowl, mix the cooked pasta with the Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce, caramelized onions, roasted cherry tomatoes, and corn.
  9. Toss everything together, then serve hot.
