Loaded Sesame Ginger Avocado Toast

Breakfast · Yield: 2 Servings

327 calories | 15g protein | 20g fat | 25g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 slices whole-grain bread, toasted
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1 red onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, finely chopped
  • 2 poached free-range eggs
  • 2 teaspoons Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame seeds
  • 1 teaspoon sea salt
  • Fresh microgreens for garnish

Directions

  1. Mash avocado and fold in the chopped red onion and tomatoes; sprinkle with salt and spread evenly on toasted bread slices.
  2. Top each slice with a poached egg.
  3. Drizzle with the Sesame Ginger Teriyaki sauce.
  4. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and garnish with microgreens.
  5. Serve immediately.
