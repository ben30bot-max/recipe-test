Spicy Garlic Parm Chicken Stir-Fry

Dinner · Yield: 1 Serving

380 calories | 32g protein | 14g fat | 35g carbs

Ingredients

  • · 4 oz. chicken breast, sliced into thin strips
  • 1 tablespoon Spicy Garlic Parm sauce
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1/2 cup bell peppers, sliced
  • 1/2 cup snap peas
  • 1 small carrot, sliced thin
  • 1/4 cup red onions, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp low-sodium soy sauce

Directions

  1. Heat half of the olive oil in a large skillet or wok over medium heat.
  2. Add the chicken strips and sauté for 6 minutes, until cooked through.
  3. Remove the chicken from the skillet and set aside.
  4. In the same skillet, add the remaining of olive oil and minced garlic.
  5. Cook for 30 seconds until fragrant, then add broccoli, bell peppers, snap peas, carrots, and onions.
  6. Stir-fry for 5 minutes until the vegetables are tender-crisp.
  7. Return the chicken to the skillet with the vegetables.
  8. Stir in the Spicy Garlic Parm sauce and soy sauce.
  9. Toss everything together, ensuring the sauce evenly coats the chicken and vegetables.
  10. Plate the stir-fry over cooked brown rice.
  11. Garnish with sesame seeds if desired.
