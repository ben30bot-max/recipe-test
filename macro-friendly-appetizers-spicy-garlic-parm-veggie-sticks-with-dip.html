MACRO-FRIENDLY APPETIZERS Spicy Garlic Parm Veggie Sticks with Dip

Dinner · Yield: 3 Servings

80 calories | 5g protein | 4g fat | 10g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 cup celery sticks
  • 1 cup carrot sticks
  • 1 cup cucumber sticks

Directions

  1. In a small bowl, mix Greek yogurt, Spicy Garlic Parm emon juice, dried dill, salt, and pepper to make the dip.
  2. Arrange celery, carrot, and cucumber sticks on a serving platter.
  3. Serve with the Spicy Garlic Parm dip.
