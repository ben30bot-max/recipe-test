Hot Honey Sriracha Baked Cod

Dinner · Yield: 1 Serving

220 calories | 23g protein | 11g fat | 8g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 oz. cod fillet
  • 1 tablespoon Hot Honey Sriracha sauce
  • 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Rub cod fillet with olive oil, paprika, and lemon juice.
  3. Place on a baking sheet and bake for 15 minutes until flaky.
  4. Drizzle with Hot Honey Sriracha sauce and serve.
