Mango Habanero Grilled Chicken & Vegetable Salad

Lunch · Yield: 2 Servings

458 calories | 51g protein | 20g fat | 17g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1 cup chopped lettuce
  • 1 cup purple cabbage
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 4 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co. Fiery Mango Habanero Sauce
  • 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • ¼ teaspoon sea salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper

Directions

  1. In a small bowl, whisk together Taste Flavor Co.
  2. Fiery Mango Habanero Sauce, fresh lemon juice, olive oil, sea salt and pepper.
  3. Grill chicken and slice thinly.
  4. In a large bowl, combine lettuce, cabbage, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, and red onion.
  5. Add chicken and drizzle each serving with 1 tablespoon of Lemon-Taste Flavor Co.
  6. Fiery Mango Habanero Sauce dressing.
