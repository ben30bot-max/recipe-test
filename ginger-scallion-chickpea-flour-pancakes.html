Ginger Scallion Chickpea Flour Pancakes

Breakfast · Yield: 2 Servings (2 pancakes each)

210 calories | 12g protein | 8g fat | 22g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup chickpea flour
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 free-range egg
  • 2 tablespoons Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce
  • 2 tablespoons chopped green onion
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • Filling: 1 cup fresh spinach, 1/4 cup shredded carrots

Directions

  1. In a bowl, whisk together chickpea flour, milk, egg, and 1 tablespoon of the Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce until smooth.
  2. Stir in green onion.
  3. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat with a little olive oil.
  4. Pour 1/4 of the batter into the skillet, swirling to form a thin crepe.
  5. Cook for 2 minutes per side.
  6. Repeat to make 4 pancakes.
  7. Sauté spinach and carrots until wilted.
  8. Divide the filling among the pancakes, roll them up, and drizzle with the remaining sauce.
