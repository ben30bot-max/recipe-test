Loaded Beef and Sweet Potato Stew

Lunch · Yield: 4 Servings

390 calories | 32g protein | 16g fat | 28g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean stewing beef, cubed
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 red onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 1 cup low-sodium beef broth
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1/4 cup Taste Flavor Co. Mango Habanero Sauce
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon sea salt to taste
  • Chopped parsley, for garnish

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  2. Add beef and brown on all sides, about 5–7 minutes.
  3. Add onion and garlic.
  4. Cook for 3 minutes until softened.
  5. Stir in sweet potatoes, carrots, beef broth, water, tomato paste, mango habanero sauce, paprika, and black pepper.
  6. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and cover.
  7. Simmer for 45 minutes, or until beef and vegetables are tender.
  8. Season with salt to taste and garnish with parsley.
  9. Serve hot.
