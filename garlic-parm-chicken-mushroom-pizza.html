Garlic-Parm Chicken & Mushroom Pizza

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

305 calories | 20.7g protein | 13.9g fat | 25.8g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 whole-wheat pizza crust
  • 1 tablespoon Spicy Garlic Parm sauce
  • 1 cup cooked chicken breast, diced into cubes
  • 1 cup mushrooms
  • 1/4 cup Kalamata olives
  • 1/2 cup red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 1/4 teaspoon sea salt

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Spread a thin layer of Spicy Garlic Parm sauce on the pizza crust.
  3. Add cooked chicken, olives, mushrooms, red onion, and Parmesan cheese.
  4. Sprinkle with sea salt and red pepper flakes.
  5. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted.
  6. Slice and serve.
