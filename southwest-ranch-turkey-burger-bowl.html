Southwest Ranch Turkey Burger Bowl

Dinner · Yield: 2 Servings

340 calories | 35g protein | 18g fat | 10g carbs

Ingredients

  • 8 oz lean ground turkey
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 avocado, sliced
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 2 tablespoons Southwest Ranch Sauce
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce

Directions

  1. Mix ground turkey with chili and garlic powder.
  2. Form 2 patties.
  3. Heat olive oil in a skillet and cook patties 7 minutes per side until cooked through.
  4. Assemble bowls with lettuce, avocado, tomatoes, and turkey patty.
  5. Drizzle with Southwest Ranch Sauce and serve.
