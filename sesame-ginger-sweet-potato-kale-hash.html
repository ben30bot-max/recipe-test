Sesame Ginger Sweet Potato & Kale Hash

Dinner · Yield: 2 Servings

295 calories | 13g protein | 13g fat | 32g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 large sweet potato, peeled and diced
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 cup diced red onion
  • 2 cups kale, stems removed and chopped
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tbsp Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add sweet potato and red onion, and cook for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until potatoes are tender and browned.
  3. Add kale and cook for 3 minutes until wilted.
  4. Create two wells in the hash and crack an egg into each.
  5. Cover the skillet and cook for 5 minutes, until egg whites are set.
  6. Drizzle with Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce and sprinkle with sesame seeds before serving.
