Southwest Ranch Breakfast Potato Skillet

Breakfast · Yield: 2 Servings

395 calories | 19g protein | 21g fat | 34g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 cups diced potatoes
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 cup diced red onion
  • 1/2 cup diced bell pepper
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • 2 tablespoons Southwest Ranch Sauce
  • ¼ teaspoon sea salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a cast-iron or non-stick skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Add potatoes, onion, and bell pepper.
  3. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 20 minutes until potatoes are crispy and cooked through.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Create 4 small wells in the potato mixture.
  6. Crack an egg into each well.
  7. Cover the skillet and cook for 7 minutes, or until the egg whites are set but the yolks are still runny.
  8. Remove from heat.
  9. Drizzle with Southwest Ranch Sauce and sprinkle with cheese.
  10. Let sit for a minute for the cheese to melt, then serve directly from the skillet.
