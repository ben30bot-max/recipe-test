"The Ranchero" Ground Turkey Pizza

Dinner · Yield: 2 servings (half a pizza each)

385 calories | 28g protein | 16g fat | 35g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 pre-made whole-wheat thin pizza crust
  • 1/4 cup Southwest Ranch Sauce
  • 4 oz lean ground turkey, cooked and crumbled
  • 2 tablespoons diced red onion
  • 2 tablespoons sliced black olives
  • 1/3 cup part-skim mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • 1 tablespoon crumbled cotija cheese
  • 1 tablespoon fresh chopped chives

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (or per crust instructions).
  2. In a skillet, cook ground turkey until no longer pink.
  3. Drain any fat.
  4. Place the crust on a baking sheet.
  5. Spread Southwest Ranch Sauce as the base.
  6. Top with cooked turkey, red onion, and black olives.
  7. Sprinkle with both cheeses.
  8. Bake for 10-12 minutes until edges are crisp and cheese is melted.
  9. Garnish with chives before slicing and serving.
