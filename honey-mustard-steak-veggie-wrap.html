Honey Mustard Steak & Veggie Wrap

Dinner · Yield: 4 wraps

400 calories | 28g protein | 22g fat | 26g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 pound flank steak, grilled and thinly sliced
  • 4 large whole-grain tortillas
  • 1 cup mixed greens
  • 1/2 cup shredded carrots
  • 1/4 cup red bell pepper, julienned
  • 1/4 cup crumbled blue cheese

Directions

  1. Season the steak with salt and pepper.
  2. Grill or pan-sear to your preferred doneness, then thinly slice.
  3. In a small bowl, whisk together Honey Mustard Sauce, olive oil, and Worcestershire sauce.
  4. Spread the sauce on each tortilla.
  5. Add mixed greens, shredded carrots, bell pepper, steak slices, and blue cheese.
  6. Roll tightly, slice in half, and serve immediately.
