Turkey Sausages with Lemon Tahini & Mango Habanero Sauce

Lunch · Yield: 8 servings

245 calories | 30g protein | 18g fat | 6g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds ground turkey
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 3/4 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1 teaspoon dried sage
  • 1 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1 teaspoon pepper
  • 4 cups of green salad for serving Lemon Tahini & Mango Habanero Sauce
  • 8 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co.

Directions

  1. In a blender, blend together all the sauce ingredients until very smooth.
  2. Refrigerate until ready to use.
  3. Mix together ground turkey, cayenne pepper, sage, ginger, salt and pepper in a bowl until well blended.
  4. Form patties from the mixture and place on a plate.
  5. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium high heat and fry the patties for about 8 minutes per side or until no longer pink in the inside and browned on the outside.
  6. Serve the turkey patties with green salad drizzled with lemon tahini.
