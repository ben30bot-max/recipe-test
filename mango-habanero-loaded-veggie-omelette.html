Mango Habanero Loaded Veggie Omelette

Breakfast · Yield: 2 Servings

274 calories | 18g protein | 19g fat | 8g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup g reen onion, chopped
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup diced red bell pepper
  • 1/4 cup chopped spinach
  • 1/4 cup diced tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons chopped red onion
  • 1/4 cup shredded reduced-fat cheddar cheese
  • 2 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co. Mango Habanero Sauce
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • ¼ teaspoon sea salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper

Directions

  1. In a bowl, whisk eggs, green onion, milk, salt, and pepper until combined.
  2. Heat olive oil in a non-stick skillet over medium heat.
  3. Add red onion and bell pepper and sauté for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Add spinach and cook until wilted.
  5. Remove veggies from skillet and set aside.
  6. Pour egg mixture into the skillet and cook undisturbed for 2 minutes.
  7. Add sautéed vegetables, tomatoes, and cheese on one half.
  8. Drizzle with 1 tablespoon mango habanero sauce.
  9. Fold the omelette over and cook for another 2 minutes until set.
  10. Slice and serve with the remaining sauce on the side.
