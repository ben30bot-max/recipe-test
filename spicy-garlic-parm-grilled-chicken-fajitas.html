Spicy Garlic Parm Grilled Chicken Fajitas

Dinner · Yield: 1 Serving

430 calories | 35g protein | 16g fat | 38g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 oz. grilled chicken breast, skinless, boneless
  • 1 tablespoon Spicy Garlic Parm sauce
  • 1/2 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1/2 green bell pepper, sliced
  • 1/2 onion, sliced
  • 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 small whole wheat tortillas
  • 1/4 avocado, sliced
  • 1 tablespoon Greek yogurt

Directions

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
  2. Grill on medium heat for 5 minutes per side, until fully cooked.
  3. Let it rest, then slice into strips.
  4. In a skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat.
  5. Add the bell peppers and onions, sautéing for about 5 minutes until tender.
  6. Add the Spicy Garlic Parm sauce to the sliced chicken and toss to coat.
  7. Warm the tortillas, then fill each with grilled chicken, sautéed veggies, avocado slices, and a dollop of Greek yogurt.
  8. Serve immediately.
