MACRO-FRIENDLY FRIES Spicy Sweet Potato Fries

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

160 calories | 0.1g protein | 3.5g fat | 31g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sliced sweet potatoes, cut into fries
  • 1 tablespoon Spicy Garlic Parm sauce
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. In a bowl, toss the sweet potato fries with Spicy Garlic Parm sauce, olive oil, sea salt, and pepper.
  3. Spread the fries on a baking sheet in a single layer.
  4. Bake for 25 minutes or until golden and crispy.
  5. Garnish with chopped fresh parsley.
