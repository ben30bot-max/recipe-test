Cherry Smoked BBQ Beef Tacos

Dinner · Yield: 1 Serving

420 calories | 32g protein | 18g fat | 38g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 oz. lean ground beef
  • 2 tablespoons Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce
  • 2 small whole wheat tortillas
  • 1/4 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1 tablespoon diced red onions
  • 1 tablespoon shredded low-fat cheddar cheese

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add ground beef and cook for 5-6 minutes until fully browned.
  3. Drain any excess fat.
  4. Stir in Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce and cook for another 2-3 minutes until the beef is well coated and heated through.
  5. Warm the tortillas and layer them with shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes, onions, cooked BBQ beef, avocado slices, and shredded cheese.
  6. Garnish with lime wedges and serve immediately.
