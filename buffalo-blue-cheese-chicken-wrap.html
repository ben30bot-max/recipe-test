Buffalo Blue Cheese Chicken Wrap

Dinner · Yield: 4 wraps

380 calories | 30g protein | 22g fat | 27g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 large whole-wheat tortillas
  • 2 cups grilled chicken breast, sliced
  • 4 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co.
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1/2 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup crumbled blue cheese
  • 1/4 cup thinly sliced red onion
  • 1 avocado, sliced

Directions

  1. Lightly warm the tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave.
  2. Spread 2 tablespoons of Buffalo Blue Cheese Sauce on each tortilla.
  3. Add sliced grilled chicken, shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes, crumbled blue cheese, sliced red onion, and avocado slice.
  4. Fold in the sides of the tortilla and roll it tightly into a wrap.
  5. Slice in half and serve immediately.
