BBQ Chicken Loaded Potato

Dinner · Yield: 1 serving

640 calories | 73g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 large russet potato (300g)
  • 170g cooked, shredded chicken breast
  • 3 tbsp (45g) no-sugar-added BBQ sauce
  • 100g 0% Greek yogurt
  • 15g shredded light cheese
  • Green onions, optional

Directions

  1. Scrub the potato and pierce with a fork.
  2. Microwave 6–8 min (or bake at 400°F for 45–60 min) until soft.
  3. While it cooks, shred the chicken and mix with BBQ sauce.
  4. Split the potato open and fluff the insides.
  5. Top with BBQ chicken, yogurt, cheese, and green onions.
