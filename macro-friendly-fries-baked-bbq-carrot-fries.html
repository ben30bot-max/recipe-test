MACRO-FRIENDLY FRIES Baked BBQ Carrot Fries

Dinner · Yield: 2 Servings

151 calories | 1.3g protein | 7.1g fat | 20.5g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 large carrots, peeled and cut into matchsticks
  • 3 tablespoons Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon sea salt
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. In a bowl, toss carrot matchsticks with Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce, olive oil, and smoked paprika until well coated.
  3. Spread the carrot fries on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
  4. Bake for 20-25 minutes or until the carrots are tender and slightly crispy, tossing halfway through for even cooking.
  5. Sprinkle with sea salt and chopped fresh cilantro before serving.
