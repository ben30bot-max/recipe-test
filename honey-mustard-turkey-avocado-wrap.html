Honey Mustard Turkey & Avocado Wrap

Dinner · Yield: 4 wraps

380 calories | 25g protein | 22g fat | 26g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 (4-ounce) turkey, grilled and sliced
  • 4 large spinach tortillas
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 cup mixed greens
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 cup shredded mozzarella cheese

Directions

  1. Season salmon fillets with salt and pepper.
  2. Pan-sear or bake until fully cooked.
  3. Flake into chunks.
  4. In a small bowl, whisk together Honey Mustard Sauce, olive oil, and Dijon mustard.
  5. Spread the honey mustard sauce on the spinach tortillas.
  6. Add mixed greens, flaked salmon, avocado slices, cherry tomatoes, and shredded mozzarella cheese.
  7. Roll the tortillas tightly and enjoy immediately.
