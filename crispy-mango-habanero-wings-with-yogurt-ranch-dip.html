Crispy Mango Habanero Wings with Yogurt Ranch Dip

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

328 calories | 30g protein | 23g fat | 5g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds chicken wings
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • ¼ teaspoon sea salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/3 cup Taste Flavor Co. Mango Habanero Sauce

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Line a baking sheet with foil.
  3. Toss wings with olive oil, paprika, onion powder, salt, and pepper.
  4. Bake for 30 minutes, flipping halfway through.
  5. Mix yogurt, lemon juice, garlic powder, dill, salt, and pepper to make dip.
  6. Toss wings in mango habanero sauce and serve with dip on the side.
