Fiery Mango Chicken and Sweet Potato Bowl

Lunch · Yield: 4 Servings

217 calories | 28g protein | 12g fat | 16g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 pound skinless and boneless chicken, diced
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 1 cup diced red bell pepper
  • 1 cup diced sweet potatoes
  • 2 red onions, sliced
  • 4 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co. Fiery Mango Habanero Sauce
  • ¼ teaspoon sea salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper

Directions

  1. Boil the sweet potato until tender.
  2. In a skillet, heat olive oil and cook chicken and red onion until golden brown.
  3. Add boiled sweet potato, red bell pepper, and spinach and stir until spinach is wilted.
  4. Stir in Taste Flavor Co.
  5. Fiery Mango Habanero Sauce, sea salt and pepper.
  6. Serve right away.
