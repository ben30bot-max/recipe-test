Buffalo Blue Cheese Turkey Burgers

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

420 calories | 37g protein | 18g fat | 30g carbs

Ingredients

  • 600g ground turkey
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup finely chopped red onions
  • 1 cup finely chopped scallions
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • 4 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co.
  • 4 whole-grain burger buns
  • 4 large butter lettuce leaves
  • 4 slices tomato
  • 4 tablespoons crumbled reduced-fat blue cheese
  • 4 red onion slices

Directions

  1. In a mixing bowl, combine ground turkey, garlic, red onion, scallions, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
  2. Form into 4 equal-sized patties.
  3. Heat a non-stick skillet or grill pan over medium heat.
  4. Cook the patties for 6 minutes per side or until well cooked.
  5. Lightly toast the whole-grain buns.
  6. Assemble the burger: • Place lettuce on the bottom bun. • Add the cooked turkey patty, a tomato slice, and red onion slice (if using). • Drizzle a tablespoon of Buffalo Blue Cheese Sauce on each patty. • Sprinkle crumbled blue cheese on top and close with the top bun.
  7. Serve immediately with a side of baked sweet potato fries or a salad for a complete meal.
