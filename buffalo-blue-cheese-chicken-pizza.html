Buffalo Blue Cheese Chicken Pizza

Dinner · Yield: 4 servings

325 calories | 28g protein | 17g fat | 22g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 pre-made whole-grain pizza crust
  • 4 tablespoons Taste Flavor Co.
  • 1 cup shredded cooked chicken breast
  • 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella cheese (low-fat)
  • 1/4 cup crumbled blue cheese
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro or parsley, chopped

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
  2. Spread the Taste Flavor Co.
  3. Buffalo Blue Cheese Sauce evenly over the pizza crust.
  4. Top with shredded chicken, mozzarella, crumbled blue cheese, and red onion slices.
  5. Bake for 15 minutes, or until the crust is crispy and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  6. Remove from the oven and sprinkle with fresh cilantro or parsley.
  7. Slice and serve.
