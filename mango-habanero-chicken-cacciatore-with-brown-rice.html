Mango Habanero Chicken Cacciatore with Brown Rice

Dinner · Yield: 6 Servings

421 calories | 34g protein | 23g fat | 10g carbs

Ingredients

  • 6 chicken thighs, skinless
  • 1 cup brown mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1/4 cup kalamata olives, pitted
  • 1 cup diced cherry tomatoes
  • 1 cup homemade chicken stock
  • 1/2 cup white wine vinegar
  • 1 carrot, peeled and chopped
  • 1 yellow capsicum, chopped
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 red capsicum, chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 4 ripe tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh oregano
  • 1/4 cup Taste Flavor Co. Mango Habanero Sauce

Directions

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. Sear the chicken for 3 minutes per side until golden.
  4. Transfer to a dish and keep warm.
  5. In the same skillet, add mushrooms and sauté for 4 minutes or until tender.
  6. Transfer to a bowl.
  7. Add the remaining olive oil to the skillet and sauté red onions for 2 minutes until softened.
  8. Stir in garlic and chopped tomatoes and cook for 4 minutes.
  9. Add chopped carrots and both capsicums, cooking for 2 minutes.
  10. Pour in white wine vinegar and let it reduce by half, about 5 minutes.
  11. Return the mushrooms and chicken to the skillet.
  12. Add olives, cherry tomatoes, chicken stock, and the mango habanero sauce.
  13. Bring to a gentle boil.
  14. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes.
  15. Stir in chopped oregano.
  16. Serve hot over a bed of cooked brown rice.
