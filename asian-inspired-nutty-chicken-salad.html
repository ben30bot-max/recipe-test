Asian-Inspired Nutty Chicken Salad

Lunch · Yield: 2 Servings

320 calories | 35g protein | 12g fat | 18g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked, shredded chicken breast
  • 2 cups shredded napa cabbage
  • 1 cup shredded red cabbage
  • 1/2 cup shredded carrots
  • 1/4 cup sliced toasted almonds
  • 1/4 cup toasted walnuts
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
  • 3 tablespoons Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar

Directions

  1. In a large bowl, combine both cabbages, carrots, cilantro, and chicken.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk together the Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce and rice vinegar.
  3. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss to combine.
  4. Top with toasted nuts and serve.
