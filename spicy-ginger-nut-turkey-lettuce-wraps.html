Spicy Ginger Nut Turkey Lettuce Wraps

Lunch · Yield: 2 Servings

300 calories | 34g protein | 14g fat | 10g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground turkey
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/4 cup diced shiitake mushrooms
  • 1/4 cup toasted cashew nuts
  • 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 teaspoon chili flakes (optional)
  • 3 tablespoons Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce
  • 1 head bibb or butter lettuce, leaves separated
  • Garnish: chopped green onion, toasted sesame seeds

Directions

  1. Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Cook turkey until no longer pink.
  3. Add mushrooms, cashew nuts, and ginger; cook for 4 minutes until mushrooms are soft.
  4. Stir in chili flakes (if using) and sauce.
  5. Cook for 1 more minute.
  6. Spoon mixture into lettuce leaves.
  7. Top with green onion and sesame seeds and fold into wraps.
  8. Enjoy!
