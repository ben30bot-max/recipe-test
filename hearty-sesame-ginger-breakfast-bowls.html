Hearty Sesame Ginger Breakfast Bowls

Breakfast · Yield: 2 Servings

325 calories | 14g protein | 18g fat | 28g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked quinoa
  • 2 large eggs, fried or poached
  • 1/2 avocado, sliced
  • 1/2 cup shredded carrots
  • 1/4 cup sliced cucumber
  • 2 tablespoons Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce
  • 1 tablespoon chopped cilantro

Directions

  1. Divide the cooked quinoa between two bowls.
  2. Top each bowl with shredded carrots, cucumber, and avocado slices.
  3. Place a cooked egg on top of each bowl.
  4. Drizzle with Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce and garnish with cilantro.
