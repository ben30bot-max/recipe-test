Cherry Smoked BBQ Veggie Burrito Bowl

Breakfast · Yield: 1 Serving

380 calories | 15g protein | 13g fat | 55g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1/2 cup black beans, cooked, drained, and rinsed
  • 1/2 cup grilled zucchini
  • 1/4 cup roasted corn
  • 2 tablespoons Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce
  • 1/4 avocado, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons salsa
  • 1 tablespoon chopped cilantro

Directions

  1. Cook quinoa according to package directions and let it cool slightly.
  2. Toss zucchini and corn with a little olive oil and grill for about 5 minutes until tender and lightly charred.
  3. In a bowl, layer quinoa, black beans, grilled zucchini, and roasted corn.
  4. Drizzle with Cherry Smoked BBQ sauce.
  5. Top with avocado slices, salsa, and cilantro, if desired.
  6. Serve immediately.
