Sesame Ginger Beef & Broccoli Stir-Fry

Lunch · Yield: 4 Servings

290 calories | 32g protein | 13g fat | 11g carbs

Ingredients

  • 1 lb flank steak, thinly sliced against the grain
  • 4 cups broccoli florets
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
  • 1/3 cup Sesame Ginger Teriyaki Sauce
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame seeds
  • 2 green onions, sliced

Directions

  1. Heat oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat.
  2. Add steak and sear for 3 minutes until browned.
  3. Remove and set aside.
  4. Add broccoli to the skillet with a splash of water.
  5. Cover and steam for 4 minutes until bright green and tender-crisp.
  6. Add garlic and ginger; stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  7. Return steak to the skillet, add the sauce, and toss to coat.
  8. Heat through for 2 minutes.
  9. Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions.
  10. Serve immediately.
