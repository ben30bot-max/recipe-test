Spicy Garlic Parm Shrimp Stir Fry

Dinner · Yield: 1 Serving

240 calories | 25g protein | 9g fat | 14g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 oz. shrimp, peeled
  • 1 tablespoon Spicy Garlic Parm sauce
  • 1 cup zucchini, sliced
  • 1/2 cup bell peppers
  • 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a skillet and sauté red onions for 5 minutes and then add zucchini and bell peppers.
  2. Cook for 4 minutes.
  3. Add shrimp and cook for 3 minutes or until opaque.
  4. Stir in Spicy Garlic Parm sauce and toss to coat.
