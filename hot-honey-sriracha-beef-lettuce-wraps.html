Hot Honey Sriracha Beef Lettuce Wraps

Dinner · Yield: 1 Serving

350 calories | 35g protein | 15g fat | 18g carbs

Ingredients

  • 4 oz ground lean beef
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • ¼ cup sliced red onions
  • 1 tablespoon Hot Honey Sriracha sauce
  • 1/2 cup shredded carrots
  • 1/4 cup diced cucumbers
  • 1/4 cup diced bell peppers
  • 1/4 avocado, sliced

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add the red onion and cook for about 5 minutes or until golden brown and fragrant.
  3. Add ground beef and cook for 10 minutes, breaking it up until browned and fully cooked.
  4. Stir in the Hot Honey Sriracha sauce, making sure the chick- en is evenly coated.
  5. Lay out the lettuce leaves, and fill each with a portion of the cooked beef, shredded carrots, cucumbers, bell peppers, and avocado slices.
  6. Sprinkle with chopped cilantro, if desired, and serve immediately.
