Grilled Lime- Mango Habanero Wings

Dinner · Yield: 4 Servings

370 calories | 29g protein | 25g fat | 6g carbs

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds chicken wings
  • 1/4 cup Taste Flavor Co. Mango Habanero Sauce
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lime juice
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • ¼ teaspoon sea salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper

Directions

  1. In a bowl, whisk together mango habanero sauce, olive oil, lime juice, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  2. Toss wings in marinade and refrigerate for 30–60 minutes.
  3. Preheat grill to medium-high.
  4. Grill wings for 15–20 minutes, turning frequently until cooked through.
  5. Brush with additional sauce before serving.
